Mayhem Companion 0.4.0 adds team-aware recommendations, personal match history, patch trends, and settings for builds and updates.

## What's new

- **Keep the companion open while playing.** Open Settings and enable “Keep companion open while playing”. F7 toggles the companion; F8 toggles the compact in-game panel.
- **Make our item builds primary.** Imported Mayhem pages go first. Recognised tool-generated pages for your current champion can be replaced while personal builds and other champions' or maps' pages are preserved.
- **Choose your build layout.** Put all build types on one item-set page or give each build type its own page. Changes made in champion select reimport your builds.
- **Team-aware bench recommendations.** Compare champion strength, teammate pairings, missing roles, and damage balance. Hover for reasons and score components. Fit points are a heuristic, not a predicted win probability.
- **Personal Mayhem history.** Import recent matches from the running League client and view results, KDA, champion damage, final items, and augments. Filter by champion or patch and compare your own champion, augment, and item results. History stays on your PC, with accounts kept separate; nothing is uploaded.
- **Patch Changes tab.** See rising and falling champions, augments, and items, with previous/current win rates, percentage-point changes, and sample sizes.
- **App updates.** Check GitHub releases on startup and every six hours, download the latest stable release, and verify its SHA-256 digest. Quit from the tray and reopen to install; the previous executable is backed up.
- **Data updates.** Refresh ARAM data automatically while the app remains open, including updates within the same patch. Settings also provides manual app and data checks. Cached data remains available offline.

## Install or upgrade

Download `mayhem-companion.exe`, put it in a writable folder of its own, and run it. If an older copy is running, choose **Quit** from its tray menu before replacing the executable. Your existing settings are retained and new settings get their defaults. Set League to **Borderless** or **Windowed** for the overlay.

Automatic app updating starts with this release. Users upgrading from 0.3.0 need to download 0.4.0 manually once.

## Validation

- Portable logic tests and the full Windows test suite pass, including history parsing and retries, account separation, build layouts and preservation, recommendation scoring, and update replacement/backup/tampering checks.
- History and Patch Changes screens have been checked on Windows.

## Notes

- Match history requires the League client to be open. It checks the latest 40 matches and accumulates saved results across sessions. Some results may be unavailable until the client finishes processing them; the app retries.
- Live Mayhem match importing and in-game item-set priority have not yet been verified against a live League session. History parsing, account separation, deduplication, retries, and recommendation logic are covered by automated tests; the History and Patch Changes screens have been checked on Windows.
- Personal samples may be small, and aggregate win-rate changes describe historical results rather than proving causation.
- App updates require a writable application folder. Installation errors are recorded under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AramOverlay\updates`.
- The executable is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn about it; a SHA-256 checksum is included with the download.
