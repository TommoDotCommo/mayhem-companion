Mayhem Companion 0.5.0 stops augment badges appearing when no augment cards are on screen, and adds a challenge tracker, history highlights and opt-in diagnostic screenshots.

## What's new

- **Badges no longer pop up over ordinary text.** A badge used to appear whenever any augment name was read on screen, so a word like "Power" in other text could trigger one. A card title now has to stand on its own, and an offer needs at least two cards before badges open.
- **Pick rounds stay correct.** Those stray badges were also counted as augment picks, which pushed later offers onto the wrong round's statistics. Only real offers count now.
- **Challenges tab.** Three sections:
  - **ARAM God**: progress toward the title, every ARAM title and how far each is, and the challenges closest to their next level.
  - **ARAM challenges**: every ARAM Warrior, Finesse and Champion challenge with a progress bar.
  - **Any mode**: challenges that aren't ARAM-specific but that Mayhem games still move, namely champion mastery and summoner level.
- **Champion checklists.** One-per-champion challenges such as All Random All Champions open into a list of the champions left and done, with a search box and Left / Done filters.
- **History highlights.** The History tab picks out patterns in your own games: how you do against everyone's win rate on the same champions, your strongest and weakest champion classes, build styles and augment types, standout augments, game length, deaths and streaks. A highlight appears only once it has enough games behind it.
- **Diagnostic screenshots (off by default).** If badges still show at the wrong time or place, turn on **Save a screenshot whenever augment badges appear** in Settings, or press **F9** in a game to capture that moment. **Create zip to send** bundles the screenshots and the log into one file for a bug report. Nothing is uploaded; you choose whether to send it.
- **Visible scroll bars.** Long pages now show a scroll bar and a fade at the bottom when there is more below.

## Install or upgrade

Versions from 0.4.0 download this update automatically; choose **Quit** from the tray menu and open the app again to install it. Otherwise download `mayhem-companion.exe`, put it in a writable folder of its own, and run it. Your existing settings are retained and new settings get their defaults. Set League to **Borderless** or **Windowed** for the overlay.

## Validation

- Portable logic tests and the full Windows test suite pass, including the new title matching, challenge parsing and diagnostic zip tests.
- The badge fix was checked in one live Mayhem game with two augment rounds: both offers were found, both picks counted, and no stray badges appeared.
- The Challenges tab was checked against a live League client.

## Notes

- If two of an offer's three card titles can't be read, no badges show for that offer; before, the one readable card would have got a badge.
- Diagnostic screenshots are of your whole screen, so they include chat and player names. They stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AramOverlay\diagnostics`, only the newest 200 are kept, and **Delete screenshots** removes them.
- The F9 capture has not yet been exercised in a live game. Change the key with `capture_hotkey` in `config.json` if it clashes with another app.
- The Any mode section assumes Mayhem games grant champion mastery and summoner experience as ARAM does.
- Challenge progress needs the League client open and can lag by up to ten minutes after a game.
- The executable is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn about it; a SHA-256 checksum is included with the download.
